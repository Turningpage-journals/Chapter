# Chapter

**Your new chapter starts here.**

First place of your own, new job, new habits. Chapter is the journal for anyone at the start of adult life. Five minutes a day for reflection, friends, love, me time and money. And one day you’ll see how far you’ve come.

Personal and private: everything you write belongs only to you.

**Open the app:** [turningpage.no/chapter](https://turningpage.no/chapter/)

Chapter is a journal from **Turning Page**. Instagram: [@turningpage_journals](https://www.instagram.com/turningpage_journals/)

## What it does

- **First start:** asks for your name, what you want to track, your currency, your activities and a colour. The app then becomes “Isabella’s chapter”, “Sophie’s chapter” and so on.
- **Today:** morning (sleep, intention, looking forward to, gratitude) and evening (mood, energy, work or day off, social, movement, self-care, money, reflection). Close the day at the end.
- **Week:** week in dots, insights and a Sunday reflection.
- **Month:** 12 monthly themes through the year, each with three reflection questions and a challenge. Plus milestones, goals per life area and a list of things you want to do.
- **Home:** home projects with costs, finds and a wish list.
- **Budget:** works in 15 currencies and with weekly, every-two-weeks, twice-monthly or monthly pay. Housing set-up for renting, owning, shared flats or living with family. Bills, debt payments, everyday spending and saving, with what’s left over, spending so far this month, an emergency-fund tracker and simple tips based on the 50/30/20 rule of thumb.
- **Patterns:** charts over time and links, for example between sleep and mood.

## Publish on GitHub Pages

1. Create a public repository called `chapter` in the `turningpage` organisation.
2. Upload all files in this folder: `index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `README.md`.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`, and press **Save**.
4. With `turningpage.no` connected to the organisation’s front page, the app is at `https://turningpage.no/chapter/`.

## Updating

Change `<meta name="app-version" content="...">` in `index.html` every time you upload a new version. Users then see “A new version is ready”. Their data stays.

## About storage

Data lives in the browser on the device. If someone changes phones or clears browser data, anything not backed up is lost. Remind users to make a backup in Settings once a month.
